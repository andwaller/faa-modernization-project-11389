Same Site, Different Schedules

What happens when thousands of project schedules are connected? Powered by Relate by Neo4j, running in your browser.

Simulated data. This page shows a method, not facts about the FAA. It makes no claim about what caused the November 13, 2026 ground stop or how much any real conflict costs.

What is this?

A government audit found that the FAA runs thousands of separate project schedules for upgrading air traffic systems. When several programs work at the same site, nobody looking at a single schedule can see the overlap. Here, simulated projects are loaded into a graph database that runs inside this page, and you can ask it questions. Choose how many projects to load: the smaller set starts fast, and the larger one matches the count GAO reported.

The larger set can take 5 to 15 seconds to load and to answer some questions.
  1. Pick a question on the left.
  2. Read the plain answer at the top of the result.
  3. Open the extra panels to see what it means, or the exact query behind it.
