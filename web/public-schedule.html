What this sample can show

These four scheduled activities are listed for EWR in the FAA's public planning report. This page loads the reported dates and project links into a fresh in-memory Relate database, then checks for date overlaps at the same facility.

An overlap is a prompt for review, not proof of a conflict or savings. The public source does not give crew assignments, qualifications, exact work locations, access restrictions, travel, or costs for these rows. The page therefore does not propose revised dates or calculate FAA savings.
